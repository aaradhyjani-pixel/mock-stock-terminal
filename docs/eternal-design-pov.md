# ETERNAL — Design POV (Stanford d.school–aligned)

## Empathize (what we observed)
- Participants sit on **laptops/desktops** and phones; desktop is the judgment station.
- Under a 25-minute clock they panic-scan OMS density and miss the **story**.
- Back-swipe / clutter / buried news felt like the product fighting them.
- They want it to feel like a **sport of judgment**, not homework in a broker skin.

## Define — Point of view
**College traders under countdown need the market’s story in one glance, a clear bet in two clicks, and immediate meaning after they act — so every decision teaches the next one.**

## Ideate → what we’re shipping as ETERNAL
1. **Brand:** ETERNAL — calm, inevitable, high-stakes clarity.
2. **ETERNAL Pulse (proprietary experience algorithm):** personal coaching on reaction timing and size quality. Never touches cash, margin, or leaderboard rank.
3. **Desktop 10x visibility:** news stage, account/rank, ticket, and Pulse moments impossible to miss at ~1280px+.
4. **Experience loops:** Sense → Story → Act → Feel → Learn (repeat each headline).

## Prototype / Test
Prototype in the live terminal; test on real devices via tunnel; refine from participant confusion, not from our taste alone.

## d.school abilities we practice here
- Navigate ambiguity (markets + rumours)
- Learn from others (team account, shared tape)
- Synthesize (news → symbols → position)
- Experiment rapidly (tiny size, then commit)
- Craft intentionally (desktop hierarchy)
