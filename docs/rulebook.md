# Participant rulebook

Read this before you sign in. Every number here comes from `config/rules.yaml`;
if the organisers change a rule, this page changes with it.

## The basics

1. **Your team starts with ₹10,00,000** of virtual cash. The team with the
   highest account value when the market closes for the last time wins.

2. **You can trade the listed stocks only**, during the timed trading days shown
   at the top of your screen. There are five days. Each is 25 minutes of trading
   with a 2-minute pre-open and a 5-minute break after it.

3. **Prices are simulated** and controlled by the organisers. They react to news,
   sometimes to rumours, and sometimes not at all. Working out which is which is
   the competition.

## Orders

4. **Four order types**: market, limit, stop-loss, and stop-limit. A limit or
   stop order that has not filled by the end of a trading day is cancelled.

5. **Large orders cost more.** Your order walks through the available liquidity,
   so a big trade fills at a worse average price than a small one. The order
   ticket shows you the estimated price before you confirm.

6. **Every trade is charged** brokerage, STT, exchange and SEBI fees, stamp duty
   and GST, at the rates a discount broker charges. A round trip on ₹1,00,000
   costs about ₹270. Over-trading loses money; the ticket shows the charges
   before you confirm.

7. **There is a rate limit** of 10 orders per team per 10 seconds, and a maximum
   of 10,000 shares per order.

## Long and short

8. **Buying uses cash only.** You cannot borrow to buy. Your holdings do not
   count as collateral, so if you spend all your cash on stock you cannot short
   until you sell something.

9. **Selling what you do not own opens a short position.** Your total short
   exposure may not exceed **5 times your account value**.

10. **If your account value falls below 12% of your short exposure**, the
    exchange automatically buys back enough of your shorts to restore a 20%
    buffer, at market, starting with your worst position. You get a warning at
    15%. There is no grace period.

11. **If your account value reaches zero**, all your positions are closed and
    your team is out. You stay on the leaderboard at ₹0.

12. **Shorts held overnight** are charged a borrow fee of 0.05% of their value at
    each day's close.

## Market conditions

13. **A stock can be halted** pending an announcement. Orders in it are rejected
    until it resumes, and it may reopen at a very different price.

14. **A stock can hit a circuit limit**, 20% above or below its opening
    reference. At the upper circuit only sells execute; at the lower circuit only
    buys. Your order on the blocked side will rest or be rejected. This is a
    feature, not a fault.

15. **A market-wide breaker** halts everything for three minutes if the index
    moves 10% in a day.

16. **A stock may pay a dividend or split**, between trading days. Neither
    changes what your account is worth. A dividend moves value out of the share
    price and into your cash, and short sellers pay it rather than receive it. A
    split changes how many shares you hold and the price of each by the same
    factor, and cancels any resting order you had in that stock, because a limit
    price set before a split means nothing after one.

## Your team

17. **All members of a team trade one account.** Up to five people can be signed
    in at once. The activity log shows who placed what, and your captain can
    reset a teammate's password.

18. **Keep your card.** If you lose your password the help desk will issue a new
    one; it cannot be recovered.

## Fair play and disputes

19. **Trades are final.** Organisers will not reverse a trade. If a technical
    fault affects you, tell the help desk with the time and a screenshot.
    Adjudication happens after the close, and any correction is made as a cash
    adjustment, recorded, and approved by two organisers.

20. **Sharing accounts between teams, running more than one team account, or
    interfering with the platform** means disqualification.

## Scoring

21. **The public leaderboard shows the top 10** and goes dark for the last 10
    minutes of the final day. Your own rank is always visible to you, blackout or
    not.

22. **Ranking is by account value** — cash, plus what your holdings are worth,
    minus what your shorts would cost to close. Open positions are not force-closed
    at the end, so nobody pays a spread just for holding at the bell.

23. **Ties** are broken by lower maximum drawdown, then fewer trades.

24. **Side awards** are computed from the same data: best risk-adjusted return,
    lowest drawdown, best single trade, best short, and highest win rate over at
    least ten trades.

25. **Everyone gets a report card.** When the competition ends, your team can see
    its own: where you finished, your equity curve, how many trades you made,
    your win rate, your best and worst trade, the largest fall you took from a
    peak, and what you paid in charges. It is on the terminal, and the
    organisers can print it.
